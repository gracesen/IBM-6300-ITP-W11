Final Shopify Store Reflection Report

Store Overview

For my final Shopify project, I created an online store called Harvest Haven, inspired by the CPP Farm Store. The store focuses on fresh produce, gift baskets, and locally made specialty products. My target customers include students, local families, and community members who are interested in fresh, locally sourced products and unique gift ideas.

One of the main goals of my store was to create a clean and welcoming shopping experience. I organized the products into collections such as Fresh Produce, Gift Ideas, and Pantry Favorites so customers could easily browse different product categories. I also designed a customizable gift basket product because I believe it offers a more convenient shopping experience than using paper order forms.

Retail Technology Reflection

Working with Shopify helped me understand that it is much more than just a website builder. It provides tools for managing products, organizing collections, customizing the storefront, processing orders, and connecting with platforms like Google Merchant Center and Google Analytics. Before taking this class, I had never built an online store from scratch, so learning how everything connects together was a valuable experience. I especially enjoyed seeing how changes I made in the Shopify admin were immediately reflected on the storefront.

Merchandising Reflection

One of the biggest things I learned was how important merchandising is in an online store. Product organization, navigation, images, and descriptions all influence the customer’s shopping experience. I spent time improving product titles, creating collections, and using high-quality images to make the store look more professional. I also learned that featuring key products, such as gift baskets, can help guide customers toward products the business wants to promote while keeping the website organized and easy to browse.

Analytics Reflection

Connecting my Shopify store to Google Analytics 4 helped me better understand how retailers can track customer behavior. Although my practice store had very little traffic, I successfully connected GA4 and confirmed that the Google tag was installed. This experience showed me how businesses can monitor page views, traffic sources, user engagement, and customer journeys to make better marketing decisions. It also demonstrated the importance of collecting accurate data before launching marketing campaigns.

CPP Farm Store Application

Building this Shopify store gave me several ideas that could benefit the CPP Farm Store. First, Shopify would allow customers to browse products and place orders online instead of relying on paper forms. Second, customizable gift baskets could be offered through product variants, making the ordering process much easier while allowing the Farm Store to update basket options based on seasonal inventory or featured products. Finally, integrating Google Analytics and Google Merchant Center would help the Farm Store understand customer behavior, improve product visibility, and measure the effectiveness of future marketing campaigns. Together, these tools could create a more convenient shopping experience while supporting both online growth and in-store sales.

Final Self-Assessment

The part of my store that I am most proud of is the customizable gift basket feature. Instead of requiring customers to complete a paper order form, they can choose different occasions and basket options directly on the product page. I think this creates a much smoother shopping experience and better reflects how modern online retailers operate.

If I had more time, I would continue improving the overall design of the website and expand the product catalog with more seasonal products and customization options. I would also like to collect more website traffic so I could analyze customer behavior in Google Analytics and use that information to continue improving the store.
